Our Capabilities

Built for Extreme Demands

Engineered for uncompromising reliability — even in the most challenging industrial and environmental conditions.

☀️

High-Efficiency Solar PV

Industrial-grade solar PV using monocrystalline PERC and HJT panels with 22%+ efficiency — designed for Bangladesh's climate and grid requirements.

  • Monocrystalline PERC / HJT panels — 22%+ efficiency, 25-year warranty
  • String and central inverters for up to MW-scale installations
  • Anti-islanding protection per IEEE 1547 / IEC 62116
  • Net metering support — BREB, DPDC, DESCO compliance
  • Remote SCADA monitoring — real-time generation, fault alerts, PR tracking
  • Bangladesh payback period: 3–5 years with 20–30% IRR
⚙️

Industrial Parts Network

Multi-brand spare parts sourcing covering mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, and pneumatic components — with emergency rapid-response logistics.

  • Multi-brand availability — local stock plus international sourcing
  • Emergency dispatch for critical production situations
  • Mechanical: bearings, seals, couplings, gears, belts, chains
  • Electrical: motors, drives, switchgear, protection relays, cables
  • Hydraulic & pneumatic: pumps, valves, actuators, cylinders
  • OEM-certified components available on request
🏗️

Full EPC Process

Single-point accountability from concept to handover — eliminating interface risk and delivering complete, operational systems under one contract.

  • FEED: Feasibility, scope definition, equipment lists, CAPEX ±15%
  • Detailed engineering: SLDs, P&IDs, cable schedules, civil drawings
  • Procurement: long-lead item management, factory inspection
  • Construction: civil, structural, installation — CPM scheduled
  • Commissioning: loop checks, relay testing, first energisation protocol
  • Handover: as-builts, O&M manuals, operator training, 12-month DLP
⚡

Smart Energy Systems

Resilient energy infrastructure integrating Solar, Grid, and Generator — managed intelligently via VoltMatrix automation platform.

  • Energy audits and load flow analysis
  • Power factor correction — reducing reactive power penalties
  • Backup power: UPS, generator, BESS battery storage
  • Grid integration and substation works — 11kV/33kV systems
  • VoltMatrix 3-source AI priority switching (Solar → Grid → Generator)
  • Real-time energy monitoring — dashboards, alerts, reporting
